LARVIP

VPN for selected websites

LARVIP is a VPN for iPhone that sends only the websites included in your link through a secure server. You create an access code, add the link you received, and connect.

Everything else keeps your normal internet connection. No account.

🔐 Access code

The access code opens the app and the VPN. It stays on your iPhone.

🔗 Add your link

Paste the link you received or scan its QR code. The camera is used only to read that code.

🟢 Connect

Turn the VPN on or off. It turns off by itself after 30 minutes without activity.

🎯 Only selected websites

Only the websites included in your link use the VPN. Everything else keeps your normal internet connection.

🎮 Incognito mode (optional)

In Settings you can create a 4-digit PIN. Entering it opens a game instead of LARVIP and turns the VPN off.
